Why Sekhon Biotech

Work that feeds
a nation.

Every mini-tuber we produce ends up in a farmer's field. That chain of consequence is short, visible, and real — and it shapes everything about how we work and who we hire.

Science you can see

Our tissue culture lab and CPRI-designed aeroponic tunnels are where the work actually happens. No abstract deliverables — you see your output harvested, counted, and shipped to farmers across India.

20 years of stability

We have been operating for over two decades through every cycle the agricultural sector has thrown at us. We don't chase trends — we build deep, long-term capability and reward people who do the same.

A team, not a number

We are a lean, hands-on team where your contribution is visible and your voice is heard. You will work closely with leadership, institutional partners like CPRI Shimla, and growers across ten states.

Life here

Serious work,

Our team is small enough that everyone's contribution shapes the outcome of the season. We collaborate closely with the Central Potato Research Institute, Shimla, and supply growers and state corporations across more than ten states.
