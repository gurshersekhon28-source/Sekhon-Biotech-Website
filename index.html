Our story

The right soil, the right climate.

Our facility is in the Shivalik foothills — a vector- and virus-free region that scientific surveys have identified as ideal for potato cultivation. The conditions matter: low disease pressure and the right soil mean fewer inputs in the field and healthier mother stock in the lab.

We commissioned our own tissue culture facility, then upgraded it with state-of-the-art aeroponics technology from the Central Potato Research Institute, Shimla. Fifteen million mini-tubers leave our facility each year, supplying commercial growers, seed multipliers, and state corporations across India.

Our standard

The Disease-Free Difference

Every potato we sell starts as a single, virus-indexed mother plant in our laboratory. From there it is multiplied through tissue culture, transitioned into our aeroponics system, and harvested as a G-0 mini-tuber — pathogen-free, traceable, and ready to multiply in your field. This is the standard we hold ourselves to. Every batch. Every season.
